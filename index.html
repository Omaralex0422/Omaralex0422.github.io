FIG. 01 — Statement

I sit at the seam between engineering and operations — the person who builds the software and also runs the business it serves.

As Manager of Digital & Operations for a dual-entity marine business, my scope runs from a native macOS app I architected from scratch, through international customs declarations and QuickBooks automation, to brand identity and AI receptionists. The same hands write the Swift and file the CBSA paperwork.

Before software there was photography — eBay product shoots for vintage ironstone and bone china, where a monochromatic eye for composition was sharpened into a permanent practice. It still governs every interface I draw.
